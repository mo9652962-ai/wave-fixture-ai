# Privacy Policy for WaveFixture AI

**Last Updated:** October 2026

WaveFixture AI is an industrial wave-soldering fixture automated design and manufacturing engine. We uphold strict **local-first** and **zero-telemetry** data protection principles.

## 1. 100% Local Manufacturing Synthesis
- All Gerber parsing (`gerbonara`), geometric contour offsets (`shapely`), DXF CAD drawing generation (`ezdxf`), CNC G-Code toolpath synthesis, and DRC design rule checks execute **completely locally** on the user's workstation.
- Proprietary customer PCB designs, panel drawings, solder mask geometries, and fixture toolpaths **never leave your local machine**.

## 2. Zero External Telemetry
- WaveFixture AI contains zero telemetry beacons, zero analytics trackers, and zero phone-home routines.
- No network requests are made during fixture generation, DRC auditing, or export procedures.

## 3. Data Ownership & Commercial Confidentiality
- All input files and generated manufacturing artifacts remain 100% the property of the engineer or manufacturing enterprise.

## 4. Contact & Security
For security or vulnerability disclosures, please review [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or open an issue at [mo9652962-ai/wave-fixture-ai](https://github.com/mo9652962-ai/wave-fixture-ai).
